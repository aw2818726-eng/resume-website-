Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This is the personal resume and portfolio website of Ahmed Walid Mohamed (“I”, “me”) in Cairo, Egypt. This page explains, in plain language, what happens to your data when you visit it.

The short version

  • There are no accounts, no forms, no comments and no ads.
  • This site sets no cookies.
  • I do not sell your data and I do not track you across other websites.
  • Files you open in the Data Cleaning Console stay in your browser. They are never uploaded.

What I collect

I do not collect personal information through this site. The only things stored are:

  • A single browser setting. When you dismiss the privacy notice, your browser’s local storage remembers that choice (key aw_privacy_notice_dismissed). It never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.
  • Aggregated visit statistics. See “Analytics” below.
  • Email you send me. If you email me, I receive your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply, and I keep the conversation only as long as needed for that purpose.

Analytics

This site uses Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. It is designed to be privacy-friendly: it does not use cookies and does not follow you across websites. It records aggregated information such as the page visited, the referring site, your country, and your device, browser and operating system type. I use this only to understand which pages are useful.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Vercel Inc. Like any web host, Vercel processes technical request data (such as your IP address) to deliver the site and keep it secure. See the Vercel Privacy Policy.

Third-party resources

  • Google Fonts. The pages load typefaces from Google Fonts. When they do, your browser contacts Google’s servers, which can see your IP address and browser details. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare). The Data Cleaning Console page loads the open-source SheetJS library from cdnjs, so Cloudflare receives a standard request from your browser. See the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.
  • Links to other sites. Links to Coursera, LinkedIn, GitHub and others take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Tools on this site

The Data Cleaning Console, Sample Size Calculator and the other demos run entirely in your browser. Anything you type or any spreadsheet you select is processed on your device and is not sent to me or to any server.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data I hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to its use. Because I hold no personal data beyond emails you send me, the practical step is simple: email me and I will help.

Children

This site is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If I change how the site works, I will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: email me.
